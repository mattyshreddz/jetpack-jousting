Jetpack Jousting

Charge down the lists. One clean stab wins a round. Win two rounds to take each bout, and beat all three knights.

Tilt your phone to fly: lean left or right to strafe, forward or back to climb or dive.
Left side is your shield: top blocks high, bottom blocks low.
Right side is your lance: top stabs high, bottom stabs low.

Line up with the enemy, stab when they’re close, and block the height their lance points at.

Your jetpack tops out around 5 feet. Dive into the dirt and you’ll faceplant: you slide face down, can’t block or stab, and a low stab will finish you. Lean up to recover.

Desktop: arrow keys fly, Z/X block, N/M stab.

Set your zero

Hold your phone the way you will play, in a comfortable neutral grip. All tilting is measured from this position.

A 3-2-1 countdown starts right after.
